    <p class="selected-project__meta">
      <strong>Team Project</strong><span aria-hidden="true"> · </span><strong>Role:</strong> Team Lead &amp; Technical Lead
    </p>
  </header>

  <figure class="selected-project__figure">
    <a href="{{ '/images/insurance-age-group-performance.png' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
      <img src="{{ '/images/insurance-age-group-performance.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Bar chart comparing insurance risk model accuracy and Macro-F1 across four age groups">
    </a>
  </figure>

  <div class="selected-project__body">
    <p>This project investigates the predictive performance, interpretability, and fairness of machine-learning models for multi-class life-insurance risk classification. Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost were trained and evaluated under substantial class imbalance. The best-performing model was subsequently examined using SHAP-based feature attribution, subgroup performance analysis, feature-ablation tests, and controlled input perturbations.</p>
